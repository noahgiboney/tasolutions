# Tooling & Assembly Solutions websites

The websites for Tooling & Assembly Solutions (TAS), a precision machining and automation machine building shop. One app serves all three sites, and each domain shows its own site:

- **[tasolutions.com](https://tasolutions.com)**: the main company site, covering our story, automation machines, quality and contact
- **[slatwasher.com](https://slatwasher.com)**: the Slat Washer, our stainless steel slat cleaning machine for the pharmaceutical industry
- **[automationdreamteam.com](https://automationdreamteam.com)**: the Automation Dream Team, the people and machines behind our automation work

For how the sites look and how to change them, see [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
